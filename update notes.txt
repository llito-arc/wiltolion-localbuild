Stats & Balance
Health & Sanity: Max health reduced (from 250 to 200), and passive sanity aura strength slightly decreased.
Starting Inventory: Replaced the starting Yellow Staff with the new Adventure Journal.
Core Mechanics
Dynamic Thermal System: Core temperature now transitions smoothly based on hunger, categorized into 4 new -internal- phases (Burning, Warm, Temperate, Extinguished).
Environmental Interaction: Rain now directly cools your core. If your core runs hot, your body will instantly evaporate wetness.
Lighting Overhaul: Wiltolion's passive glow has been redesigned with realistic light layers and better performance optimization.
Progression & Skills
Skill Tree: Brand-new custom progression tree with 14 unlockable skills, granting access to exclusive recipes, passive cooking, and new perks.
Magic & Lunar Affinity: New passive skill tree perks that reduce the durability or sanity costs of magic staves and lunar equipment.
Favorite Food: Banana Juice is now officially Wiltolion's favorite food (replacing Dragonpie).
Equipment & Items
Sun Torus Buffs: The shield is now easier to activate (requires 3 hits with a more forgiving time window) and provides minor weather/wetness protection. Crafted ones lose character restrictions, letting you gift them to teammates (fixing the character-swap exploit).
Sundrops: Unstable drops of light that fall from the sky. They evaporate if left on the ground and will burn any other player who tries to pick them up.
Sun Flower: A new plant grown from Sundrops. It provides an AoE of warmth, passive sanity, and a dim light. Picking it deals burn damage to non-Wiltolion players, and it naturally withers away after 30 real-world minutes.
New Structures & Menus
Wiltolion Pylon: A new craftable structure that lets you fast-travel across the map via a custom interactive interface.
Adventure Journal & Radial Menu: Reading the journal now opens a quick on-screen radial menu to command and control Wilto’s,Actions.
HUD Polish: Added custom visual badges to track core heat and lunar magic. The annoying red overheating screen border has also been disabled for Wiltolion.
Summons & Companions
Echo of Wilto: Summon Wilto's silhouette! He has his own stats and can be healed using standard medical items.
Sun Spider: A new summonable creature made of light that acts purely as a healing and buffing ally.
Wiltolion Buddy: Tiny swarm allies you can summon. They automatically attack resource-stealing pests (like Gobblers and Buzzards) as well as anything you attack or that attacks you.
Immersion & Fixes
Dialogue Expansion: Hundreds of new speech lines for Wiltolion, a complete compendium biography, and new unique examination quotes from vanilla characters when they inspect you.
Skins API Compatibility: Built-in support to be 100% compatible with the Modded Skins API mod.
MiM Compatibility: Built-in support to ensure 100% compatibility with the Mods in Menu mod.
Sprite & Texture Polish: Several textures have been improved, including Wiltolion's titles and character sprites, for a cleaner and more polished look.
General Bug Fixes: Fixed the misaligned position of the Sun Torus, alongside numerous other unlisted bug fixes and tweaks that I totally forgot to note.

[h2] 🦁 MANE FEATURES [/h2]

☀️ [b]SOLAR REACTOR[/b]
Wiltolion's innate heat consumes hunger 25% faster, but in return, he constantly radiates warmth, light, and a small sanity aura. 
As a solar lion, he is completely immune to fire and environmental overheating. 
Conversely, he is highly vulnerable to cold and water. A well-fed core can endure freezing temperatures and dampness, but if his stomach empties, his internal reactor will fail and he will begin to freeze—even in summer!
Wiltolion is fast and strong, but as the sun sets, his strength fades. He becomes significantly weaker at night or inside the Caves, so tread carefully!

🌕 [b]MOON FRIEND[/b]
As a living celestial body, his nature is deeply aligned with the moon, and he gets along well with other stellar entities.

⚫ [b]ENEMY OF SHADOWS[/b]
Shadows are his worst enemy. He fears the dark, and taking hits from shadow monsters actively drains his sanity. Wearing shadow equipment will also take a heavy toll on his mind.
